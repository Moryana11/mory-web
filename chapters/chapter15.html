Chapter XV. – Whispering currents and the shadow in the sand

Rowena awoke suddenly, as if something were calling to her. Silence filled the tent. The orb inside her bag glowed faintly, as though it too could sense the summons. Slowly, she reached for it and lifted it into her palm. Its light brightened slightly, responding to her touch. Carefully, she rose to her feet, draped her cloak over her shoulders, and slipped past the sleeping Aaliyah. The tent flap rustled softly behind her. Again, she heard the voice — closer now, clearer. It guided her farther into the oasis, beyond the fig trees. To an ancient sanctuary. Rowena approached, her heart beating with a strange rhythm. The orb grew warm in her hand, shining with a golden-white radiance. She stepped inside. In the coolness of the stone chamber, she felt something like a breath—a gentle current of air brushing against her neck. At the center of the sanctuary lay a spring. Crystal-clear water swirled peacefully within a small stone basin. Rowena knelt beside it, placed a hand upon the rim, and bowed her head. And then the water spoke. "I remember you, Child of Light." The voice did not come from the air around her. It echoed directly within her mind. She remained calm. "Why did you call me?" she whispered. "I do not call. I answer. You asked the question, even if not with words. And I know what you seek." "Morr'kalil." She spoke the name again—the name that had become a thorn lodged within her dreams. "His name is older than the sands of this land. His hunger endures." The water rippled. Images emerged upon its surface: ancient gates, corrupted temple walls, figures cloaked in black, pupil-less eyes staring from the depths, and a silent call echoing across the ages. "Where must we go?" Rowena asked. The orb in her hand shone brighter. "To the place where stone drank blood and swords raised walls. To the place where human pride stood against the gods. To the place where the First Seal trembled." The voice faded, but the ripples upon the water continued to move, forming a pattern — a map. Not a complete map, but a guide. Rowena leaned closer, her eyes tracing the shifting lines on the water's surface. "That's a castle..." she murmured. Then certainty settled within her. "Stonegard castle." Her voice carried a confidence born not from knowledge, but from instinct. Quietly, Rowena rose to her feet, tucked the orb beneath her cloak, and returned to the tent. Now she knew where they had to go.
