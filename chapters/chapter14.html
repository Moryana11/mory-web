Chapter X. – The warning

The ancient temple looked exactly as it had before. Cracked pillars leaned beneath the weight of centuries. The stone floor was littered with rubble, and the walls were scarred by deep fissures. And yet, something had changed. The shadows still lingered among the stones and within the empty alcoves, but they no longer seemed quite as threatening. The oppressive feeling that had gripped the temple like an invisible fist had weakened. Aaliyah carefully looked over Rowena and James. Only after reassuring herself that neither of them was injured did she feel a sense of relief. She had no idea what they had gone through, but they were here. Alive. Together with her. She slowly exhaled. “So, we made it?” Aaliyah asked. Rowena paused thoughtfully and glanced around the temple. “I thought we did,” she finally replied. “But we're still here. The shadow has retreated, but it hasn't disappeared.” James frowned as his gaze swept across the crumbling walls. “So we're looking for another key?” “Exactly,” Rowena said with a nod. “Something has changed, but it doesn't seem like the temple is ready to let us leave yet.” Suddenly, a middle-aged woman emerged from the darkened corners of the temple. Her light brown hair, threaded with a few silver strands, was neatly gathered into a smooth bun. Her pale green eyes shone with wisdom and calm. “Welcome,” she said, her voice steady and clear. “Congratulations on completing the trial.” The woman slowly stepped closer, her gaze lingering on all three of them. “My name is Lysara,” she introduced herself quietly, though there was unmistakable authority in her voice. “I was a healer of the Tower of Pure Light who helped seal Morr'kalil nearly five hundred years ago—a being whose darkness almost consumed our world.” Her words echoed through the temple like remnants of a distant age. “I created the Fragment of Time in which you now stand as a warning for those who would come after us. The beginning of a new era is approaching—an era in which you must be stronger, wiser, and more united than ever before. Each of us has a role to play in this game of fate. Your journey is only beginning.” Lysara's expression grew more serious, and concern darkened the depths of her green eyes. “I must warn you,” she began slowly. “Morr'kalil's bonds are weakening. His darkness yearns to return, and if it succeeds, it will bring ruin upon the entire world.” From a small chest, she withdrew a tiny orb that glowed with a soft, muted light and handed it to Rowena. “This is a Sealing Orb,” she explained. “It contains a portion of the magical power that keeps Morr'kalil bound. You must protect it, find the remaining three, and one day use all of them together to seal Morr'kalil once more should his darkness attempt to break free.” “Wait,” Aaliyah breathed. “Are you telling us that the fate of the entire world depends on four orbs?” “And on us?” Rowena added. James shook his head. “That doesn't make any sense. There are thousands of mages, knights, and healers in the world. Why us?” Lysara met his gaze calmly. “Because fate has chosen you.” James frowned. “That isn't an answer.” “Unfortunately, the time I have left grows short. I cannot remain here any longer, nor can I give you more information. You must rely on yourselves—your strength and your courage are now your only weapons against the darkness that approaches.” Her voice softened into a whisper, as though she were saying farewell to the little time she had left. “Remember that you are not alone. The light can shine again, if you stand firm and act together.” With one final look filled with wisdom and a silent blessing, Lysara vanished as quietly as she had appeared. The temple began to tremble, as though the Fragment of Time itself had recognized that its purpose had been fulfilled. The walls, motionless until now, shuddered violently. The light lingering in the corners began to fade, and the silver runes carved into the stone slowly vanished. The Fragment of time— Lysara’s final creation— was falling apart. Instinctively, Aaliyah pressed herself against James, seeking comfort in his presence amid the chaos unfolding around them. His arm immediately wrapped firmly around her. Without hesitation, he extended his other arm and pulled Rowena close as well. He shielded both of them with his own body. He was not a knight, nor had he ever pretended to be a hero. But he knew one thing. If something struck, it would hit him first. And then...everything disappeared.
