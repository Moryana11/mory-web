Chapter XI. – Return

The harsh sunlight struck their eyes. Aaliyah instinctively narrowed her eyes and took a deep breath. Dry, scorching air filled her lungs, carrying the scent of sand and relentless heat. Beneath her feet, she felt the burning ground, nothing like the cool stone floor of the temple. The last traces of Lysara's magic faded away, taking with them the silence of that ancient place. They were back. In the middle of the desert. James was still holding both women in his arms. For a moment, they remained exactly as the Fragment of Time had left them, until they were certain that the desert surrounding them was not another illusion. Rowena was the first to carefully pull away and look around. The dunes appeared unchanged, but the air felt heavier, charged with tension. "We're back," she stated simply. Aaliyah knelt down and scooped a handful of sand, letting it slip through her fingers. It was warm. Burning hot. Real. "It almost feels like nothing has changed," she said quietly. But when she looked at the glowing orb in Rowena's hand, she knew everything was different. James swept his gaze across the horizon, the shadows of the dunes, and the sunlight shimmering off the scorched earth. "I don't know how much time has passed," he muttered. "But if Morr'kalil's bond is weakening, we need to act quickly." Rowena nodded. "We need to find the nearest shelter, get a map, and then determine where the remaining orbs are located." Aaliyah rose to her feet and brushed the sand from her clothes. "We'll head east. The Sanctuary Oasis is a day's journey from here. We'll find everything we need there—assuming thirst doesn't claim us first." Without another word, they set off across the dunes toward the oasis.
