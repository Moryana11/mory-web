Chapter XVII. – Where the water leads

The sound of the tent flap announced Rowena's return. Her face was paler than usual, and her movements were quiet, as though she were returning from a place where no one spoke aloud. Aaliyah sat wrapped in a light blanket, while James stood nearby with his arms crossed. "The water knows me," Rowena said softly, yet with complete certainty. "It speaks to me." Aaliyah shivered. "What did it say?" "That evil is awakening, and the answers do not lie here. It showed me part of a map." James frowned. "Part of a map? Did you recognize the place? Did it tell you anything else?" Rowena shook her head. "No. It said nothing more. We have to go to Stonegard Castle. That's where we'll find the answers." At that moment, Harim entered the tent. His expression was grave. Slowly, he looked from Aaliyah to James and then to Rowena, as though weighing how best to tell them what he had just learned. Aaliyah pulled the blanket closer around herself. James remained motionless, his face tense, his eyes fixed on Harim. Rowena stared at him intently, the orb clenched tightly in her hand. Harim took a deep breath. "I need to tell you something." For a moment, he fell silent. "The patrol sent to relieve two young guards at the northern gate found one of them dead. He was torn to pieces. His eyes were wide open, as if the terror he felt before he died had been frozen there forever." Harim rubbed his temples, and for an instant his gaze lost focus, drifting into the darkness of memory. "And the other boy..." he continued quietly, "was standing over the body. But he wasn't the same young man they had known. The patrol barely recognized him—not because of the blood covering him. His appearance had changed so completely that he seemed like a stranger, something impossible. His skin was black as coal. His eyes were gold—like molten metal. There was nothing human in them, only emptiness and an icy calm. He wore black armor covered in spikes, and he carried a sword and a spear." Harim lifted his eyes. "He never spoke. He simply looked around him." "Then he slowly turned and stepped into the shadows." A chill settled over the tent. "He didn't leave." Harim's voice dropped to little more than a whisper. "He simply vanished." Silence settled over the tent. Aaliyah pulled the blanket tightly around her shoulders and stared into the shadows gathered in the corner. Her hands trembled slightly, though she tried to remain composed. "How is that possible?" she finally whispered. "How can someone change like that?" "I don't know," Harim replied. "I've never seen anything like it." James frowned. For a moment, he said nothing. Then he lifted his head. "We've encountered it before." Harim turned to him in surprise. "Encountered what?" "That shadow." Aaliyah looked at James but allowed him to continue. "We came across it before we reached the oasis. It threatened us with death." James clenched his jaw. "But we had no idea it could do something like this." "Turn a person into... that?" Harim asked quietly. James nodded. "If we'd known, we would have warned you." Harim was silent for several moments. Then he slowly shook his head. "Don't blame yourself." James looked up at him. "Even if you had told us, it wouldn't have changed anything." "How can you know that?" Aaliyah asked. Harim rubbed his forehead tiredly. "We are healers, pilgrims, and guardians of the oasis. We know how to drive off bandits, survive in the desert, and care for the wounded." His voice grew heavier. "But this... this is something entirely different." He looked at all three of them. "None of us possesses the knowledge or the power to stand against something capable of transforming a human being into such a creature." Rowena tightened her grip on the orb. Harim continued. "Perhaps we would have sent more guards. Perhaps we would have closed the gates for a few nights." He shook his head. "But that wouldn't have solved the problem." Aaliyah lowered her gaze. "That's why Stonegard castle is our best chance." Harim nodded. "Exactly." His gaze moved from James to Aaliyah and finally to Rowena. "If there are people anywhere who understand what you're dealing with, you'll find them at Stonegard castle." "I hope you're right," Aaliyah said quietly. "So do I." Harim fell silent for a moment. "And I'm hoping for one more thing." "What?" Rowena asked. Harim glanced toward the tent entrance, beyond which the night stretched endlessly. "That you reach Stonegard castle before that shadow finds you again."
