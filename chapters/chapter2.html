Chapter II. – Sands of the Moonlight desert

Several weeks had passed since they left the ruins of Turayne behind, and their journey had eventually led them into the unforgiving Moonlight Desert. The sun hung in the sky like a blazing disk. The sand beneath their feet crackled and burned with every step. Rowena was sweating beneath her dark blue robe, whose fabric had long since lost its original luster. The water in her waterskin was running low. Her magic shielded her from the worst of the heat, but even magic had its limits. Still, she did not complain and kept moving forward. Beside her, Aaliyah practically skipped across the sand dunes as if she felt no exhaustion at all. "Hey, if we happen to find a desert spa out here, I'm signing up for a sand wrap and cactus therapy!" she called out enthusiastically just as they came upon the skeleton of a giant scorpion. "Though I'd definitely like to skip the scorpion massage." Rowena could only laugh. When night finally fell and the moon illuminated the dunes until they resembled silver waves, they made camp between two rocky outcrops. Aaliyah started a fire with a flick of her finger, while Rowena brewed tea from desert herbs she had gathered earlier. They sat beside one another. Silence stretched around them, broken only occasionally by the distant howl of the wind. "You know most people go mad out here within three days?" Aaliyah asked, staring into the flames. "Because they hear voices," Rowena replied quietly. "The desert forces them to relive their most painful memories and confront the fears they've spent their lives running from." Aaliyah smiled faintly. "Do you hear anything?" Rowena hesitated. Then she nodded. "I hear myself. All my doubts. All the times I could have saved someone and didn't make it in time." "Those aren't the desert's voices," Aaliyah said, turning toward her. "That's your conscience. And yours is so enormous you could probably smother an entire continent with it." Rowena let out a sigh. "And you?" she asked. "Do you hear anything?" Aaliyah fell silent. But the silence did not last long. Something began to rise from the sand beneath their feet—something large, heavy, and covered in scales. Rowena reacted instantly, spreading a protective barrier around them. A Sand Lizard emerged from the ground, a monstrous creature with a maw full of needle-like teeth and eyes like two black pearls. The moment its body cleared the sand, it lunged forward. Its jaws opened wide, and with a terrifying hiss, it slammed directly into the glowing shield. A deep impact rang out, like stone striking metal. The magical barrier flared brightly, and a wave of energy hurled the beast backward into the sand. The lizard hissed, clawed at the ground, and rose again in fury. Rowena clenched her fingers, her focus sharpening. "Now!" she shouted. The shield surrounding them faded, dissolving into golden sparks. Aaliyah was already moving. She leaped forward, her blazing staff gripped tightly in her hand. With a single sharp gesture, she conjured a pulsing fireball before her, bright and radiant as a miniature sun. The sphere shot forward, superheating the air around it. But the Sand Lizard was faster than either of them expected. At the last instant, it twisted sharply aside. Its massive body plunged into the sand, and the fireball streaked past its head. The resulting explosion blasted sand in every direction. The beast roared again and charged without hesitation. This time, it was aiming directly for Aaliyah. Its jaws snapped shut on empty air, rows of teeth glinting like needles eager to tear flesh apart. Aaliyah darted aside, her flaming staff still in hand. "Stupid lizard," she hissed. She was fast. With a swift flick of her wrist, she gathered magic into her palm once more. The fire surrounding her spiraled inward and, within a heartbeat, condensed into another blazing fireball. She launched it straight at the beast. The Sand Lizard tried to dodge again. Too late. The fireball struck it directly in the head. A sharp explosion echoed across the dunes, and flames poured over its scales like a wave of molten heat. The beast screamed in agony. Its body convulsed violently. Its tail lashed wildly through the air, and a harsh, rasping shriek erupted from its throat. The scorching air trembled as its scales blackened and cracked beneath the intense heat. Then the lizard collapsed. Cooked. The magical flames slowly died away, fading into thin streams of smoke. The air remained hot and heavy, thick with the smell of burning flesh. Rowena and Aaliyah stood several paces from the dead creature, breathing hard. Neither of them took their eyes off it. Its massive body lay motionless upon the sand. Still, they waited. Tense. Ready. Prepared to strike again if it moved. Several long seconds passed. Nothing. Only the wind stirred the sand around the creature's charred scales. When it finally became clear that the beast would not rise again, the tension slowly drained from their bodies. The flames surrounding Aaliyah faded completely, and her shoulders relaxed slightly. Rowena immediately stepped over to her and gave her a quick, practiced inspection from head to toe. She gently touched Aaliyah's arm, as if checking for pain or some hidden injury. "Are you alright?" she asked softly. Aaliyah nodded. Her breathing was still quick, but her gaze remained steady. "Yeah. Not even a scratch." She laughed. "I'm starting to think you're cursed, Rowena. Ever since I met you, I haven't had a single peaceful day." "Maybe I am," Rowena replied. "And you're crazy for sticking around." Aaliyah smiled. "Or maybe I've just gotten used to the chaos." When they settled back down beside the dying fire, neither of them spoke. Then, in the distance beyond the dunes, a moving shadow appeared. And with it came a deep, rhythmic sound. Footsteps. "That's not an animal," Aaliyah said quietly. Rowena nodded. "No. It's something else." Without another word, they rose to their feet and silently watched the shadow slowly drawing closer.
