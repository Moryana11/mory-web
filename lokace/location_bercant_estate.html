Bercant estate

The Bercant estate was once one of the most prominent noble estates in the region, renowned for the fine fabrics produced by its skilled craftsmen. Its glory, however, came to an end after the death of Duke Bercant. His children, Limuny and Yonhark, began to fight over control of the family inheritance. As the conflict gradually divided the servants, soldiers, and residents of the surrounding settlements, Limuny resolved to gain power at any cost. In a forgotten section of the estate's cellars, she discovered a being known as Norn—once human, now twisted by an ancient curse that transformed living creatures into grotesque plant-like monstrosities. Norn offered Limuny power in exchange for freedom. When the pact was sealed, the curse spread throughout the entire estate. People began to disappear or transform into horrifying creatures entwined with wood, thorns, and flowers. The Knights of the Thorned Flower abandoned their humanity and swore loyalty to Norn in exchange for superhuman strength. Within a matter of weeks, the once-prosperous estate became a place of death. Today, the Bercant Estate is an abandoned ruin consumed by wild vegetation. Roots grow through its stone walls, thorn-covered vines hang from its windows, and the once-grand halls have become dark corridors overrun with plants. Deep within the estate wander deformed servants, cursed knights, and other victims of the ancient catastrophe. The local people avoid the estate at all costs. It is said that the curse has never truly faded and that anyone who ventures too deep into its depths risks sharing the same fate as the members of House Bercant. Few possess the courage to enter the heart of the estate, where, according to legend, the remnants of the forces that once doomed the entire domain still linger.
