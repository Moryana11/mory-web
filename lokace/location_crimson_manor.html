Crimson manor

Crimson Manor belongs to the prestigious Crimson family. Its wealth comes primarily from the breeding and sale of high-quality riding and war horses, which are highly sought after by nobles, merchants, and knights from across the region. On the surface, the estate appears to be a prosperous and well-managed domain. Its vast stables, paddocks, and agricultural buildings are meticulously maintained, and commercial activity never truly ceases. In reality, however, a far darker truth lies hidden behind its walls. Deep beneath the main manor is a network of secret chambers accessible to almost no one. Children brought from various corners of the land are held there and subjected to dangerous magical experiments. At the same time, they undergo years of relentless and merciless training designed to transform them into perfectly obedient and deadly assassins. Only members of the Crimson family, their most trusted servants, and a handful of high-ranking nobles from Herba are aware of these activities. These nobles make use of the Crimson family's services for tasks that cannot be carried out openly—from espionage and sabotage to the assassination of political rivals. Through mutual benefit and carefully guarded secrets, the true nature of Crimson Manor remains hidden from the outside world.
